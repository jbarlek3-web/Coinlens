Cited Sources

Values are estimates derived from published price-guide methodology (Sheldon grading scale, Red Book-style tiered pricing, and metal-content floors). Actual market value depends on authentication, third-party grading (PCGS/NGC), eye appeal, and current auction results. Consult a licensed numismatist or submit to a grading service for high-value coins.
CoinLens uses the Sheldon 1–70 grading scale, published price-guide conventions, and precious-metal spot floors. Data references: PCGS Price Guide, NGC Price Guide, A Guide Book of United States Coins ("Red Book"), Numista, USA Coin Book, Heritage Auctions archives.
